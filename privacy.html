Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

EventRows is a Google Sheets™ add-on that creates and updates Google Calendar™ events from spreadsheet rows. It runs inside your Google account on Google's Apps Script platform.

What we access and why

  • The current spreadsheet (spreadsheets.currentonly): to read the rows you choose to sync and to write back the link between each row and its event. EventRows cannot open your other files.
  • Calendar events (calendar.events): to create, update and delete the events that correspond to your rows, and to import events into a sheet when you ask for it.
  • Your calendar list (calendar.calendarlist.readonly): to show the calendars you can choose from.
  • Sidebar display (script.container.ui) and external requests (script.external_request): the only external request checks your license key with our payment provider, Creem.

What we store

Your spreadsheet data and calendar events stay in your Google account. EventRows does not copy, sell or share them, and does not store them on our servers. Settings (chosen calendar, column mapping) are stored in the spreadsheet's add-on properties; your license key and monthly usage counter are stored in your Google user properties.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Creem, our merchant of record, under its own privacy policy. We receive your email address and order details to deliver and support your license.

Google API Services User Data Policy

EventRows' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising and do not allow humans to read it, except with your explicit permission for support, for security purposes, or to comply with law.

Removing access

Uninstall EventRows from Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons, and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Events already created stay in your calendar until you delete them.
