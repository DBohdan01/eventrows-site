FAQ

What date and time formats work?

Real date cells in any format, plus text like 2026-09-25, 25.09.2026, 9/25/2026, 18:30 or 6:30 PM. A time can be in its own column or in the date cell.

What happens if I delete a row?

On the next sync the matching event is deleted (you can switch this off). Rows with errors are never deleted — you see the row number and the reason.

Can I bring existing events into a sheet?

Yes: “Import events from a calendar” creates a new sheet. Edit it and sync back.

Where is my data stored?

In your spreadsheet and your calendar only. EventRows runs inside your Google account and does not copy your data to our servers. See the Privacy.

Does it sync automatically?

Today you press Sync (or use the menu). Hourly auto-sync is next on the roadmap.
