The service

EventRows creates, updates and deletes Google Calendar™ events based on rows in your Google Sheets™. You are responsible for the content of your sheets and for choosing the calendar that events are written to. Deleting rows with “Delete calendar events when their row is removed” enabled deletes the matching events.

Plans and payment
